Implementation choices, NOT claims of equivalence:
- Mainline names core,bus,ppu are required; no StarFive register aliases.
- Retains boot-established PPU domain, inherited frequency and DFS.
- Checks power status and timing; refuses initialization if inconsistent.
- Does not alter regulator voltage, PLL rate or reset ordering.
- ActivePM and DVFS intentionally disabled during bring-up; firmware internal
  power islands enabled. System suspend explicitly returns failure rather than
  claiming unimplemented resume works.
- Pre/post callbacks keep platform domain powered. The empty post callback is
  deliberate pinned-power policy, not hardware-emulation success.
- Actual GPU submissions, firmware load, interrupts and memory management are
  provided by the real vendor driver, not emulated/stubbed.
- Hardware-dependent DMA/cache coherence and boot-retention assumptions are
  unresolved until device tests. This candidate is not the finished OS.

Build validation:
 sysconfig.o compiled against actual Linux7.3 ARM64 headers with GCC14.3,
 strict kernel warnings and no platform-warning suppressions.
 Full module validation is coordinated with generic-driver work.

Boot/resource prerequisites:
- Mainline U-Boot may never initialize the GPU PPU as vendor boot0 does.
  Do not assume a fresh SD-only mainline boot meets the retained-state gate.
- clk_disable_unused can alter inherited clocks before module loading.
  Diagnostic clk_ignore_unused may preserve evidence but is not a production
  power solution. Capture PPU/DFS and clock state before/after kernel transition.
- MMIO PPU/DFS ownership currently uses ioremap, not a shared regmap/domain
  provider. Do not enable this driver alongside another PPU owner. A production
  power-domain provider and managed resource ownership remain required work.

Inherited-state preflight guards (2026-10-23):
- Before clock enables or PPU/GPU register reads, inspect always-on CCU
  GPU_BGR0x0300167c and R-CCU PPU_BGR0x0701017c bit16 reset release.
  Addresses/bits are from Linux7.3 ccu-sun50i-a100{,-r}.c reset maps.
- After PPU clock enable, require PPU internal reset+gate bit0 set, both
  domain isolation bits clear, status3D, control modeHW2 and power-request
  IRQ bit0 clear. These are vendor pm_domains.c plus
  ppu_mode_switch(POWER_CTRL_MODE_HW,false) prerequisites; no writes added.
- SW mode is rejected because this glue lacks the domain-B request IRQ
  handler and acknowledgement. HW-mode acceptance is only a precondition,
  not proof of GPU firmware functionality or board power sequencing.
- Pinned reference: gpu-driver/port/reference/allwinner-4.9-pm_domains.c,
  original Creality7f37a7fbf4ad59907034715e3d967b9177de1cd4.
- DFS mapping/read is postponed until PPU validation and GPU clocks enabled.
