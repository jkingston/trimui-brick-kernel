2026-11-09 stopped-off correction
--------------------------------
Exact reference: stock TG3040_Brick_firmware_v1.1.1.zip rootfs,
/lib/modules/4.9.191/pvrsrvkm.ko, SHA256 above. AArch64 disassembly of
sunxiPostPowerState, 0x86d20..0x86da8, masks PPU request IRQ (+0x2c bit 0),
selects SW=1 (+0x24 low two bits), reads back, then writes HOST=1
(+0x20 low two bits). HOST is protocol metadata, not independent proof of
firmware/IRQ quiescence. The platform erroneously waited for HOST first,
latching non-forced OFF failures even after successful firmware stop.

The verified non-forced path now consumes same-transaction firmware-stop
evidence, drains the GPU IRQ synchronously, validates accessible PPU/DFS/clock
state, and executes the stock SW/HOST -> internal ALLOFF ordering. Existing
forced/unknown-state handling remains separate. Readbacks and fault latching
are retained; no retry follows a failed destructive transition.

A second device iteration captured reset=1 gate=1 isolation=0 status=1
control=2 mask=0. HOST may precede 3D isolation. Accept this powered tuple
only in the stopped-off path; do not weaken wake/general ownership checks.

Host regression: tools/testing/selftests/sunxi_gpu/ppu_stop_test.c mocks
MMIO, verifies exact write ordering and preserved bits, rejects inaccessible
states, and injects failed SW/HOST writes before any physical power cut.

Device: matching kernel/modules, vendor 1.19@6345021 UM and stock firmware,
GE8300 BVNC 22.102.54.38. Final revision passed 120 measured 250 ms live-context
idle/wake cycles across three process invocations, followed by all-pixel
retained-texture checks; six additional idle warmups are excluded from that
count. Pango-shaped texture coverage also matched the CPU oracle. KMS and
process restart/recovery tests are recorded in the custom-firmware repository's
docs/display-runtime-validation.md. Driver/firmware status and all fault/HWR
counters were clear; dmesg was checked for the separately latched PM failures.
This is bounded bring-up evidence, not production or suspend qualification.
